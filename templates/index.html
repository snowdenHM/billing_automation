Tally & Zoho Books automation

Bills in. Vouchers out.

BillMunshi reads a scanned bill the way your accounts clerk would — vendor, invoice number, dates, line items, GST — puts it in front of whoever has to approve it, and posts the finished voucher into Tally or Zoho Books. No retyping, and nothing reaches your books that a person did not sign off.

What it actually does

Six things, each of which exists because somebody was doing it by hand.

Reads the bill

Upload a PDF, a photo or a whole scanned batch. OCR and a vision model pull out the vendor, bill number, dates, line items, taxes and totals. Multi-bill scans are split into separate documents.

Checks itself

Line totals against the invoice total, GST splits against the rate, the vendor against your existing ledgers. What does not add up is flagged on the bill before anyone approves it.

Routes it for approval

A bill moves from draft to analysed to verified to synced, and the step that matters is a person's. Every change is kept in a per-bill activity log with who and when.

Posts to Tally

Purchase, sales, expense, payment and receipt vouchers go straight into Tally through a connector, against the parent ledgers and GST ledgers you have mapped — including reverse charge and TDS.

Posts to Zoho Books

The same bill can go to Zoho Books instead: vendor bills, expenses and journal entries, matched to your Zoho vendors, chart of accounts and tax codes over the Zoho API.

Keeps clients apart

Each organisation is its own workspace with its own ledgers, bills and credentials. Members are Admin, Manager, Accountant or Consultant, and switching client is one menu away.

Four steps, and you only do two of them

Upload and approve. The rest is the product's job.

Upload

Drop in bills one at a time or as a batch. Duplicates of something already in the system are caught at upload.

Extraction runs

The document is read in the background and comes back as a filled-in voucher with its line items and tax split.

Review and approve

Correct anything the model got wrong, pick ledgers where it was unsure, and approve. Flagged checks are shown beside the field they concern.

Sync

The voucher is posted into Tally or Zoho Books. If the target rejects it, the bill stays put with the reason attached.

Who it's for

Anyone who is currently typing invoice data into Tally or Zoho Books by hand.

In-house finance teams

You run one set of books and the bills arrive faster than anyone can key them in.

  • One workspace for purchase, sales, expense, payment and receipt vouchers.
  • An approval step so nothing posts without a reviewer.
  • An audit trail per bill: who changed what, and when it synced.

Accounting firms and consultants

You run books for several clients and each one is a separate Tally company.

  • A workspace per client, with its own ledgers and credentials.
  • Roles per client — give a consultant read-only access without giving them everything.
  • Switch clients from the top bar instead of signing out.

There is an API behind all of it

Everything the web app does, it does over a documented REST API. Organisations authenticate with scoped API keys, so your own tooling can upload bills and read back vouchers.
